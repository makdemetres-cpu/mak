4 · Legal bases

Where I rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time by emailing me; withdrawing does not affect anything done before you withdrew. Where I rely on a legitimate interest, I have weighed it against your rights and you can object at any time (see §9).

5 · How long I keep it

  • Booking enquiries that do not become projects — 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client project correspondence and files — for the length of the engagement and 5 years after, in line with the general limitation period for contractual claims.
  • Invoices and accounting records — the retention period Greek tax law requires (currently 5 years from the end of the relevant financial year); I cannot delete these on request.
  • Published reviews — until you ask me to remove them, which I will do without asking why.
  • Consent records — as long as the consent is relied on, plus 5 years.
  • Server logs — as set by the hosting provider, typically no more than 30 days.

  • the hosting provider for this website — [provider name, country];
  • the email provider that carries our correspondence — [provider name, country];
  • if you use it, the video-call provider for the consultation — [provider name, country];
  • my accountant, for invoices, and public authorities where the law requires it.

7 · Transfers outside the EEA

I choose EU-based providers wherever there is a reasonable one. If a provider processes data outside the EEA, the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with additional safeguards, as required by Chapter V of the GDPR. Ask me and I will tell you exactly which providers are involved and on what basis.

The typefaces on this site are served from this website's own server, not from a third-party CDN, so simply reading a page does not disclose your IP address to anyone else. There are no social media pixels, no embedded videos and no advertising trackers. The Instagram link is an ordinary link — nothing loads from Instagram until you choose to click it.

8 · Cookies and local storage

Everything non-essential is off until you switch it on. The detail — what is stored, for how long, and how to change your mind — is in the Cookie Policy. You can reopen the choices any time from Cookie settings.

9 · Your rights

Under Articles 15–22 GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data I hold about you, and get a copy;
  • correct anything inaccurate or incomplete;
  • erase it ("right to be forgotten"), where no legal duty makes me keep it;
  • restrict processing while a dispute is sorted out;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • not be subject to automated decision-making — there is none here.

Email hello@sitrixweb.com and I will answer within one month, free of charge. I may ask a question to confirm it is really you, and nothing more than that.

10 · Complaints

If you think I have handled your data badly, tell me first — I would rather fix it. You also have the right to complain directly to the supervisory authority:

11 · Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Form data is transmitted encrypted, access to the mailbox is protected with two-factor authentication, and only I have access to it. No security is absolute, but if a breach ever put your rights at risk I will notify the authority within 72 hours and tell you directly, as Articles 33–34 require.

12 · Children

This site is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 15 (the age of digital consent under Greek Law 4624/2019). If you believe a child has sent me data, email me and I will delete it.

13 · Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes affecting how your data is used will be flagged on the site, and where consent is the basis, asked for again.
