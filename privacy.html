01Who we are

In this policy, “we”, “us” and “Ermis' Villas” mean , a registered in Greece, with its registered seat at , VAT number and General Commercial Registry (GEMI) number .

We are the data controller for the personal data described here. That means we decide why it is collected and what happens to it, and we are the ones answerable to you and to the supervisory authority for it.

We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer under Article 37 GDPR, and we have not appointed one voluntarily. Data protection questions are handled directly by the family, at .

02What this policy covers

It covers personal data we handle when you use this website, when you enquire about or book one of our estates, while you are staying with us, and afterwards. It applies whether you contact us through this site, by email, by telephone or through WhatsApp.

It does not cover other companies' websites we link to, or the independent businesses you may deal with directly during your stay, such as a restaurant that takes your own reservation. Those organisations are controllers in their own right and have their own policies.

03What we collect

Information you give us

  • Identity and contact details: name, email address, telephone number, and the country you are travelling from.
  • Enquiry and booking details: which estate, arrival and departure dates, the number of adults and children, the services you would like, and anything you write in the notes field.
  • Arrival details: whether you are coming by air or by sea, the airport or port, the airline and flight number or the ferry line, and the time you expect to arrive — where you have asked us to meet you. We do not check these against any airline or ferry system; they are simply what you have told us.
  • Details of the other guests in your party, where you give them to us (see §6).
  • Preferences and requirements: dietary requirements, allergies, accessibility needs, occasions, and anything else you choose to tell us so that we can get the stay right (see §5).
  • Correspondence: the content of emails, messages and notes of telephone calls.

Information we need in order to comply with the law

  • Contract and payment records: the rental agreement, invoices, and the record of amounts paid. We do not take card payments through this website and this site never receives your card details.
  • Guest and property records that Greek law requires an accommodation provider to keep and, in some cases, to report — including information connected to the AADE Short-Term Residence Property Registry and to accommodation levies.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and referring page, to the extent our hosting provider records it in server logs for security and reliability.
  • Your cookie choice, and any analytics data you have consented to (see §9).
To confirm — CCTV If any of the four estates has cameras covering an entrance, a gate or a perimeter, that is personal data processing and it must be described here: where the cameras are, what they cover, how long footage is kept, and the legitimate-interests basis relied on. Guests must also be told on arrival. If there are no cameras anywhere, replace this box with a plain statement saying so.

05Health and dietary information

An allergy, an intolerance, a medical condition or an accessibility requirement is a special category of personal data under Article 9 GDPR, and gets extra protection.

We only ever hold this because you have chosen to tell us, so that the chef does not poison somebody and so that the house works for everyone staying in it. We rely on your explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a). We share it only with the specific person who needs it: the chef, or the driver arranging a wheelchair-accessible vehicle. Never more widely.

You can withdraw that consent at any time by telling us, and we will delete the information. If you withdraw it before your stay we may not be able to cater safely for the requirement, and we will say so plainly rather than take the risk.

06Other people in your party

If you give us details about other guests, such as names, ages of children and dietary requirements, you are responsible for making sure you are entitled to share them, and for telling those people that we have them and pointing them to this policy.

We use those details only to arrange and deliver the stay, keep them for the same periods set out in §10, and any of those people can exercise the rights in §11 directly with us.

08Transfers outside the EEA

We keep personal data inside the European Economic Area wherever we can, and we choose suppliers on that basis.

Where a supplier does process data outside the EEA, we only allow it where the European Commission has decided that country offers adequate protection, or where the transfer is covered by the Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses together with any additional safeguards the transfer needs. You can ask us which mechanism applies to a particular supplier and we will tell you.

09Cookies and this website

Under Article 4(5) of Greek Law 3471/2006, which implements the e-Privacy Directive, we may only store information on your device, or read information already stored there, if it is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for — or if you have consented.

So nothing optional is loaded until you choose. The banner appears each time you open the site, refusing is a single click in exactly the same place as accepting, and you can change your mind at any time through Cookie settings in the footer of every page.

Things this site deliberately does not do

  • No third-party fonts. The typefaces are served from our own server, not from Google Fonts, so no request carrying your IP address is made to a third party just to render text.
  • No embedded maps. The chart of the four locations on our contact page is a drawing, not a map service, so no tile provider ever learns that you visited.
  • No social plugins, no embedded video, no tag manager.
  • No profiling and no cross-site tracking of any kind.

About the link you arrived on

The ev_campaign entry above deserves a plain explanation, because it is the one thing here that sounds like tracking. If you reach us through a link we have tagged, the tag is read from the address bar and held in your browser for that visit. If you then send an enquiry, it travels with it, so we know which post or newsletter brought you. If you do not send an enquiry, it is discarded when you close the tab and we never see it. It is never shared, never combined with anything else, and it does not follow you to any other website.

Your browser can also block or delete storage for this site directly, in its own settings. Blocking strictly necessary storage will not stop you reading the site, but the cookie banner will ask you again every time and the booking form will not be able to keep a draft.

11Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 GDPR you have the right to:

  • Be told what we hold about you and why, which is what this document is for.
  • Get a copy of your personal data (Art. 15).
  • Have it corrected if it is wrong or incomplete (Art. 16).
  • Have it erased where we no longer have a good reason to keep it (Art. 17). We cannot erase what tax law obliges us to retain, and we will tell you when that is the case.
  • Restrict what we do with it while a dispute about it is resolved (Art. 18).
  • Receive it in a portable format, or have it sent to another controller, where we hold it on the basis of consent or contract (Art. 20).
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21). If you object to direct marketing, we will stop, always and immediately.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we relied on it. That does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these, write to . It is free. We will reply within one month; if the request is complex we may extend that by up to two further months, and we will tell you within the first month if that happens (Art. 12(3)). We may need to confirm who you are before releasing personal data, and we will ask for the least we can get away with.

13Security

We take the measures Article 32 GDPR requires, proportionate to a business of our size: this website is served only over HTTPS; access to booking records is limited to the three of us; devices are password-protected and encrypted; suppliers are put under written confidentiality obligations; and we do not keep what we do not need.

We never ask for card details by email or over the telephone, and we never will. If you receive a message that appears to come from us asking for card or bank details, treat it as fraudulent and call us on the number on this site.

If a breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Hellenic Data Protection Authority within 72 hours as required by Article 33, and tell you directly without undue delay where Article 34 requires it.

14Children

This website is meant for adults, and we do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. We do receive details about children travelling in a party (first names and ages, so that cots, stair gates and pool fencing are in place) from the adult making the booking, who is responsible for providing them.

Under Greek Law 4624/2019 the age at which a child can consent to information society services on their own is 15. Nothing on this site is offered to children on that basis.

15Automated decisions and profiling

We do not make any decision about you by automated means alone, and we do not profile you. Every enquiry is read and answered by a person, which is the whole point of how we work.

16Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the version and date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use personal data we already hold, we will tell affected guests directly rather than relying on you to notice.

18Reviews you publish

Editor's note — resolve before launch Two things here need a decision rather than a paragraph. First, whether reviews are moderated: js/data.js → REVIEWS.moderated is currently false, so a review appears the moment it is written. Second, and related: since 2022 a trader who displays consumer reviews has to say whether and how they check that the reviews come from people who actually used the service. The paragraph below says what the site does today. If you start verifying that reviewers really stayed — which is the safer position — rewrite it to describe that instead.

If you leave a review on our homepage, the name you type and the words you write are published on that page for anyone to read. That is the point of them, but it does mean you are choosing to make that information public, and it is why the form asks you to agree before it will publish anything.

What we ask for, and what we do not

A name and the review itself. Nothing else — no email address, no account, no rating of you. A first name is genuinely enough, and if you would rather appear as “A guest, Athens” then type that. We store the review, the rating and the time it was written.

How reviews are checked

Reviews on this site are not currently verified as coming from guests who stayed with us. Anyone who visits the site can leave one. We say so plainly because Article 7(6) of the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive, as amended in 2019 and implemented in Greece through Law 2251/1994, requires a trader who displays consumer reviews to state whether and how they ensure the reviews come from consumers who have actually used the service. Claiming otherwise would be a misleading practice, and we would rather tell you than claim it.

The legal basis

Your consent, under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, given by the checkbox on the form. You can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing it does not affect the lawfulness of what we did with it before you did.

Having a review removed

Email with roughly when you left it and we will take it down. You do not have to give a reason, and we will not ask for one. This is the same right to erasure described in §11; it is repeated here because a review is the one thing on this site you can publish yourself, and you should not have to hunt for how to unpublish it.

Who else sees it

Everyone — it is on a public web page, and search engines will index it. Reviews are stored by our database provider, described in §7 among the other processors. We do not pass them to review aggregators or advertising networks.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove it, or until we take the review section down. Unlike the booking records in §10 there is no accounting period forcing us to keep it.
