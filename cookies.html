Cookie Policy.

Last updated 8 September 2026 · Greek Law 3471/2006 (Art. 4(5)), Directive 2002/58/EC and the GDPR

This site is close to cookie-free by design. Here is everything it stores, without exception.

1 · The short version

No advertising cookies. No social media pixels. No third-party analytics running behind your back. Until you make a choice in the banner, the only thing stored is the record of that choice itself — which is strictly necessary and permitted without consent under Art. 4(5) of Law 3471/2006.

2 · Exactly what is stored

These are entries in your browser's local storage, not classic cookies — they are never sent to a server, and never leave your device.

NameCategoryWhat it holdsLife
sitrix_consent_v1Strictly necessaryYour cookie choice per category, with a timestamp, so the banner stops askingUntil you clear site data or change your choice
sitrix_my_reviews_v1PreferencesA copy of a review you submitted, so you can still see it while it waits to be published. Local to your device; other visitors never see it.Until cleared; only the last 5 are kept

Analytics and marketing appear as switches in the settings panel but load nothing today — the site ships with no analytics provider configured. If that ever changes, it will only run for people who have switched it on, and this table will be updated first.

3 · Third parties

Fonts are served from this site's own server, so reading a page does not reveal your IP address to a font CDN. There are no embedded maps, videos or social widgets. The Instagram button is a plain hyperlink: nothing is requested from Instagram unless you click it, at which point Instagram's own privacy policy applies.

Your hosting provider necessarily sees the request that delivers the page — that is covered in §3 of the Privacy Policy.

4 · Changing your mind

Open Cookie settings — from here or from the footer of any page — and switch categories on or off. Withdrawing is exactly as easy as consenting, which is the point.

5 · Browser controls

You can also clear or block storage for this site in your browser's settings (usually under Privacy → Cookies and site data). Doing so removes your saved choice, so the banner will ask again on your next visit.

6 · The law behind this

Storing or accessing information on your device requires your prior, informed, freely given consent unless it is strictly necessary for a service you asked for. That comes from Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC, transposed in Greece by Art. 4(5) of Law 3471/2006, with consent measured against the GDPR standard (Art. 4(11) and 7). Consent must be as easy to withdraw as to give, and pre-ticked boxes do not count — so there are none here.
