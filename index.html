The family

Ermis bought the first house in 2019 and let it out himself: met the guests at the gate, carried the bags, cooked when the cook cancelled. Seven years later there are four houses, and the only real change is that his two sons answer the phone as well.

Ermis and his sons

We have four houses. All of them are ours, and between the three of us we have slept a night in every bedroom in all of them. There is no portfolio behind us and no regional director above us.

It is why we can be specific. The water pressure on the top floor of Villa Kyma is excellent. The road down to the cove at Thalassa is steeper than it photographs. We know because we have been in both houses in February, with nobody watching.

Just over a hundred and fifty guests have stayed since 2019. Most come back, several every summer, and a few now text Ermis about dates before we have opened the year. We would rather stay this size than grow into something with a switchboard.
