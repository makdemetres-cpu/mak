Our Story

A word the dictionary can't quite hold.

Meraki (μεράκι) has no direct translation — it means doing something with so much love and attention that you leave a piece of yourself in it. That's the whole idea behind this small coffee house on a marble side-street in Plaka.

We roast in small batches, bake before sunrise, and set every table the way we'd set one for family. No rush. No shortcuts. Just good coffee, good light, and the Acropolis keeping watch over the terrace.
