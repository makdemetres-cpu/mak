The family

Ermis bought the first house in 2019 and let it out himself — met the guests at the gate, carried the bags, cooked when the cook cancelled. Seven years later there are four houses, and the only real change is that his two sons answer the phone as well.

Ermis and his sons

We are not a management company. We do not have a portfolio, a regional director, or a hundred houses we have never slept in. We have four, all of them ours, and between the three of us we have stayed a night in every bedroom in all of them.

That is the whole proposition. When we tell you the water pressure on the top floor of Villa Kyma is excellent, or that the road down to the cove at Thalassa is steeper than it photographs, it is because we have been there in February with nobody watching.

Just over a hundred and fifty guests have stayed with us since 2019. Most of them have come back, several of them every summer, and a few now text Ermis directly about dates before we have opened the year. We would rather keep it that way than grow into something that needs a call centre.
