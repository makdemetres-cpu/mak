05 · Process

How a project runs

Four steps, no surprises

  1. 01 Step 01 — Call

    A free 30-minute call

    You tell me what the business needs to happen. I tell you honestly whether a website is the way to get it, and roughly what it costs. No deck, no pitch.

  2. 02 Step 02 — Scope

    A written scope and a fixed price

    Every page, every feature, what is not included, and the dates. You approve it before anything starts, and it does not move unless you ask it to.

  3. 03 Step 03 — Build

    Build in the open

    You get a live preview link from week one and a short update every Friday. Feedback goes in as we go, not in one painful round at the end.

  4. 04 Step 04 — Launch

    Launch, then hand over the keys

    Speed, accessibility and GDPR checks before go-live. Then every account, password and file is transferred to you, with a walkthrough recording you keep.
